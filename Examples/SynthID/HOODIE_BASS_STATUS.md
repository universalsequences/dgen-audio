# SynthID Hoodie Bass Status

Target: the ten-note Korg Monologue set tagged `hoodie bass` in eseq's
`crates/sequencer/samples.db`. C2 was used for topology development; the first
sample to cross the requested 70% success threshold is the G#2-labelled target:

```text
9e9b21934c4afaa227ce80636fb8680bcf8b908a77353e969a74f0585bc82268.wav
```

The library uses an octave convention in which this C2 is 32.7 Hz (scientific
pitch C1). All ten files are near two seconds, 44.1 kHz stereo with effectively
identical channels, and tagged `monologue SOUND`, `instruments`, and
`Resampled`.

## Measurement

- Fundamentals: C2 measures 32.64-32.75 Hz; winning G#2 measures about
  52.05-52.15 Hz and is steady after the onset.
- Peak: 0.2285; DC: 6.4e-6; stereo max difference: 3.05e-5.
- Envelope: roughly 100-200 ms rise, long sustain, release beginning around
  1.5-1.6 s. The other nine notes show the same shape.
- The steady C spectrum has a strong third harmonic (about -2 dB from the
  fundamental), second harmonic around -8 dB, and progressively weaker upper
  partials. The first 300 ms is brighter than the sustain.
- Provenance: resampled Korg Monologue sample-library audio; source losslessness
  beyond the stored PCM WAV is unknown.

`scripts/analysis/analyze_hoodie_bass.py` reproduces the housekeeping, pitch,
envelope, and harmonic measurements from the eseq library.

## Voice

Profile: `--profile hoodie-bass`.

- 32 integer-locked additive partials, each with sine and cosine scalar
  coefficients.
- Three zero-default partial banks use 1x, 2x, and 4x one fitted exponential
  decay to model slow, medium, and fast attack-localized brightness.
- Smooth differentiable attack/decay/sustain/release envelope.
- Softsign output stage with fitted drive and gain.
- 8 kHz analysis/render rate. This preserves 32 partials through the tagged
  set's highest measured note while giving the 2048 window 3.9 Hz resolution
  and staying inside the Metal FFT threadgroup-memory limit.

No samples, target-derived tables, FIR taps, or residual waveforms enter the
patch.

## Validation

- Swift/NumPy renderer parity on the winning G#2 patch: max abs error
  2.80e-5 (threshold 1e-3).
- Finite-difference relative errors: attackTime 8.1e-5, noteOff 1.93e-3,
  brightnessDecay 3.33e-4, representative harmonic h3s 2.22e-4.
- `f0` fails fdcheck (about 0.47 relative error even at epsilon 1e-4), consistent
  with the existing swept-pitch adjoint exception. The profile freezes `f0` at
  the robust CPU steady-pitch estimate and does not use that gradient.
- C2 topology development reached 68.20% but did not cross 70%.
- G#2 uses a baseline regenerated by the current Swift pipeline, not a
  transferred run's cold start. Independent `compare.py` distance falls from
  0.2092139 to 0.0616191: **70.5473% improvement**. `compare_70.json` reports
  `pass: true` with `requiredImprovement: 0.7`.
- This clears the requested 70% target. It does not redefine or claim the
  separate 80% kick-paper acceptance gate.

Winning artifacts: `output/rung3_hoodie_bass_gs2_v1/`; authoritative baseline
and prepared target: `output/rung3_hoodie_bass_gs2_baseline_audit/`.

## eseq port

The recovered patch is ported as
`crates/sequencer/instruments/bass/synthid-hoodie-bass/`. MIDI pitch replaces
the fitted G#2 fundamental, and the fixed sample note-off is mapped to a normal
gate-aware ADSR. `instrument_probe` passes at both A4/44.1 kHz and the fitted
G#2/8 kHz with finite output and healthy peak/RMS. The instrument UI displays
the winning 70.55% result and has a checked-in headless capture fixture.
